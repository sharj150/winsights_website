Straight answers

Do I need any experience?+

None at all. Many members join knowing nothing about markets. Your onboarding call and the Welcome tab point you to exactly where to start, and the curriculum builds from there at your pace.

What’s the difference between the plans?+

Only how you pay. Every plan includes everything on this page: the newsfeed, the briefings, the trade ideas, the Winsights Portfolio, the curriculum, Winsights AI and 1:1 support.

Will I make money?+

We can't promise that, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Most retail traders who use leveraged products lose money. What we can promise is that you'll understand far more about why markets move, and make your own decisions with your eyes open.

Is this financial advice?+

No. Winsights provides education and research. We don't know your circumstances and never tell you what to do with your money. For personal advice, speak to a regulated financial adviser.

Do you post trade ideas?+

Yes. Every trading day we share FX and gold trade ideas, each with the setup, the reasoning and the level where the idea is wrong. They're there to teach you how a trade is built, and you decide what, if anything, to do with them. Trading leveraged products is high risk and most retail traders lose money with them.

Why WhatsApp?+

It's the app you already check. The day-to-day happens in five focused groups, and everything that needs a proper home (guides, the Portfolio, your tracker, Winsights AI) lives in your member portal.

What happens after I join?+

You'll get a WhatsApp welcome with your community invite and an email to set your portal password. We’ll also book your one-to-one onboarding call, and the Welcome tab tells you exactly where to begin for your level.

How do I cancel?+

Any time, from your billing settings. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

Do you earn from broker links?+

Yes, and we'd rather tell you than hide it. We have referral partnerships with some brokers and may earn a commission if you open an account through our links. You never need to use them to be a member.
