About Winsights

A UK research and education desk that makes the markets easy to understand.

Winsights is a UK research and education desk for investors and traders. We explain what’s moving the markets in plain English, share daily trade and investment ideas with the reasoning behind them, and help members learn as they go, on WhatsApp and in their own member portal.

At a glance

  • Who we are: Winsights, also known as Winsights Markets, a trading name of Winsights Group Limited.
  • Company: registered in England and Wales, company number 16813424.
  • Founder: Sharjeel Khatri (SHARJ).
  • Based in: the United Kingdom.
  • What we cover: UK and US stocks, ETFs, forex, gold (XAU/USD), crypto, macroeconomics and central banks.
  • Who it’s for: complete beginners, long-term ISA and SIPP investors, day traders and experienced investors.
  • Where: five WhatsApp desks (News, Markets, Stocks, Crypto and Chat) and the Winsights member portal.

What members get

  • The Winsights newsfeed: market-moving headlines on WhatsApp the moment they break.
  • An 8am morning briefing, and daily FX, gold and equities trade ideas with key levels.
  • The Winsights Portfolio, with every position logged alongside its thesis and outcome.
  • A curriculum and guide library in PDF and video, from beginner to advanced.
  • Winsights AI, a research assistant for terms, charts and headlines.
  • An onboarding call for every new member, and 1:1 learning support.
  • The free Winsights Open Desk newsletter every Sunday.

How we work

We’re not a blind-follow signals group. Our ideas are built on fundamentals: economic data, central bank policy, and events like geopolitics and supply shocks. We show our working, including the trades that go wrong, so members learn why, not just what.

From the founder

“After years spent engrossed in markets and the financial space, one thing became clear to me: market intelligence shouldn’t belong only to institutions. Everyone deserves quality financial research, market transparency and the confidence to make their own informed decisions.”

Sharjeel Khatri, Founder

What we’re not

Winsights is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority and does not give personal financial advice. Everything we publish is for education. For advice on your own circumstances, speak to a regulated financial adviser.
